# 09_BUSINESS_RULES.md

# Part 1 — Core Business Rules, Product Lifecycle & Inventory Management

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the business rules that govern the HOPSY PLAZA e-commerce platform.

Business rules are mandatory system behaviors that must be enforced by the backend regardless of frontend implementation.

They apply to:

* Customer Website
* Admin Dashboard
* APIs
* Database
* Background Jobs
* Payment Processing
* Inventory Management

Frontend validation must never replace server-side enforcement.

---

# 2. Guiding Principles

Every business rule should prioritize:

* Data Integrity
* Security
* Consistency
* Auditability
* Scalability
* Maintainability

When conflicts occur, backend business rules always take precedence over client-side behavior.

---

# 3. Product Status Rules

A product may exist in one of the following states:

* Draft
* Published
* Archived

Rules:

* Draft products are invisible to customers.
* Published products appear on the Customer Website.
* Archived products cannot be purchased.
* Archived products remain in the database for historical records.
* Products should never be permanently deleted during normal operations.

---

# 4. Product Visibility Rules

A product is visible only when:

* Status is Published.
* At least one active variant exists (if variants are enabled).
* At least one product image exists.
* The product belongs to an active category.
* The assigned brand is active.
* Required pricing information is available.

Products that fail these conditions must not appear in search or browsing results.

---

# 5. Product Variant Rules

Products may contain multiple variants.

Examples:

* Color
* Storage
* RAM
* Size
* Edition

Rules:

* Each variant must have its own SKU.
* Each variant maintains independent inventory.
* Variant pricing may differ from the parent product.
* Variant images may differ.
* Customers must select required variants before purchase.

---

# 6. SKU Rules

Every sellable product or variant must have a unique SKU.

Requirements:

* Automatically generated by default.
* Editable by administrators when necessary.
* Must remain unique.
* Cannot be reused by another active product.

SKU uniqueness must be enforced at the database level.

---

# 7. Barcode Rules

Products may include barcodes.

Rules:

* Barcodes must be unique.
* Optional for product creation.
* Searchable within the Admin Dashboard.

Barcode changes should be recorded in audit logs.

---

# 8. Serial Number Rules

Products requiring serial numbers should support:

* Unique serial assignment
* Serial validation
* Inventory association

A serial number cannot be assigned to multiple inventory items.

---

# 9. Brand Rules

Rules:

* Products must belong to exactly one brand.
* Brands may contain unlimited products.
* Archived brands cannot receive new products.
* Existing products retain historical brand associations.

---

# 10. Category Rules

Rules:

* Products must belong to at least one category.
* Categories may contain unlimited products.
* Archived categories cannot receive new assignments.

Category hierarchy should remain consistent across navigation and search.

---

# 11. Pricing Rules

Each product or variant may include:

* Regular Price
* Sale Price

Rules:

* Sale Price must never exceed Regular Price.
* Prices cannot be negative.
* Currency is Nigerian Naira (₦).
* Price calculations occur only on the server.

The client must never determine the final payable amount.

---

# 12. Inventory States

Inventory should distinguish between:

* Available Stock
* Reserved Stock
* Incoming Stock
* Out of Stock

Each state must be calculated accurately from inventory records rather than manually maintained where possible.

---

# 13. Stock Reservation Rules

Inventory should be reserved when:

* Checkout begins (if reservation is enabled).
* Payment is being processed.
* An administrator manually reserves stock.

Reserved inventory is unavailable for purchase by other customers.

Reservations must automatically expire after a configurable timeout if payment is not completed.

---

# 14. Inventory Adjustment Rules

Administrators may adjust stock for reasons including:

* Restocking
* Damaged Goods
* Manual Correction
* Returns
* Supplier Deliveries

Every adjustment must generate an inventory movement record.

No inventory adjustment may occur without an associated reason.

---

# 15. Inventory Movement History

Every inventory movement must record:

* Product
* Variant
* SKU
* Movement Type
* Quantity
* Previous Stock
* New Stock
* Administrator
* Timestamp
* Reason

Inventory history must be immutable after creation.

---

# 16. Low Stock Rules

Administrators should configure a low-stock threshold per product or globally.

Rules:

* Products reaching the threshold generate alerts.
* Alerts should appear on the Admin Dashboard.
* Alerts should not prevent purchases while stock remains available.

---

# 17. Out-of-Stock Rules

When available inventory reaches zero:

* Customers cannot add the product to the cart.
* Customers may still view the product.
* Similar products should be recommended.
* Administrators receive inventory alerts.

The system must prevent overselling.

---

# Part 2 — Customer, Cart, Checkout, Orders, Payments & Commerce Rules

---

# 19. Customer Account Rules

Customers may:

* Register
* Login
* Checkout as Guest
* Save Addresses
* Save Payment Cards (tokenized only)
* Create Wishlists
* Compare Products
* Track Orders
* Download Invoices
* Submit Reviews

Rules:

* Each email address must be unique.
* Each phone number may only be associated with one customer account.
* Customers cannot create duplicate accounts using the same authentication method.
* Customer accounts must remain active unless explicitly suspended by an administrator (future-ready capability).

---

# 20. Authentication Rules

Supported authentication methods:

* Email & Password
* Google
* Email OTP
* Phone OTP

Rules:

* Email verification is required for registered accounts.
* Passwords must never be stored in plaintext.
* Authentication is handled through Supabase Auth.
* Backend authorization must validate every protected request.

---

# 21. Address Rules

Customers may store multiple addresses.

Rules:

* Only one default billing address.
* Only one default shipping address.
* Address validation is required before checkout.
* State and city are mandatory.
* Phone number is required for delivery.

---

# 22. Wishlist Rules

Rules:

* Wishlist items belong only to the owning customer.
* Duplicate products should not appear multiple times.
* Wishlist items remain available until manually removed or the product is archived.
* Products can be moved directly from the wishlist to the shopping cart.

---

# 23. Compare Rules

Rules:

* Customers may compare compatible products.
* Duplicate products are not permitted.
* Comparison data should persist for authenticated users.
* Product specifications must be compared using standardized attributes.

---

# 24. Recently Viewed Rules

Rules:

* Maintain a limited history of recently viewed products.
* Display the most recent items first.
* Recently viewed products are customer-specific.
* Guest users may use browser storage for temporary history.

---

# 25. Shopping Cart Rules

Rules:

* Cart items belong to either a customer account or a guest session.
* Products must exist and be available before being added.
* Selected variants are mandatory where applicable.
* Quantity cannot exceed available stock.
* Price calculations always occur on the backend.
* Archived products cannot remain purchasable.

---

# 26. Cart Price Calculation

The backend must calculate:

* Product Total
* Discount
* Shipping
* Tax (if applicable)
* Coupon Value
* Grand Total

The frontend must display values returned by the server.

---

# 27. Checkout Rules

Checkout requires:

* Customer Information
* Delivery Method
* Billing Address
* Shipping Address
* Payment Method

Rules:

* Products must still be available before order creation.
* Stock validation occurs immediately before order creation.
* Final pricing is recalculated during checkout.

---

# 28. Payment Rules

Supported methods:

* Paystack
* Bank Transfer
* Cash on Delivery

Rules:

* Payment verification occurs on the backend.
* Webhooks update payment status.
* Duplicate payment processing is prohibited.
* Payment references must be unique.
* Failed payments must not create completed orders.

---

# 29. Cash on Delivery Rules

Rules:

* Available only where configured by the administrator.
* Orders remain unpaid until delivery confirmation.
* Inventory is reserved according to inventory policies.

---

# 30. Bank Transfer Rules

Rules:

* Customers receive payment instructions after placing the order.
* Payment confirmation requires administrator verification.
* Orders remain pending until payment verification is complete.

---

# 31. International Shipping Rules

For international customers:

* Orders may be submitted before shipping costs are finalized.
* Administrators manually calculate shipping costs.
* Customers must approve final shipping charges before payment completion (if additional payment is required).

---

# 32. Order Status Rules

Allowed statuses:

* Pending
* Paid
* Processing
* Delivered
* Cancelled
* Refunded

Status transitions must follow valid business workflows.

Invalid transitions should be rejected.

---

# 33. Order Cancellation Rules

Rules:

* Customers may cancel only before processing begins (if the feature is enabled).
* Administrators may cancel orders when appropriate.
* Cancelled orders restore reserved inventory where applicable.
* Cancellation events are recorded in audit logs.

---

# 34. Invoice Rules

Invoices must:

* Have a unique invoice number.
* Reflect the final confirmed order values.
* Be downloadable by customers.
* Remain immutable after issuance, except where legally required.

---

# 35. Coupon Rules

Coupons support:

* Expiration Date

Rules:

* Expired coupons are rejected.
* Coupon validity is checked during checkout.
* The backend determines whether a coupon is applicable.
* Invalid coupons must return clear validation messages.

Future enhancements may include usage limits, category restrictions, and brand restrictions without requiring architectural changes.

---

# 36. Promotion Rules

Supported promotions:

* Flash Sales
* Bundle Discounts
* Buy One Get One
* Seasonal Campaigns

Rules:

* Promotions may have scheduled start and end dates.
* Promotions cannot produce negative pricing.
* Promotion eligibility is evaluated server-side.
* Overlapping promotion conflicts must follow predefined priority rules.

---

# 37. Review Rules

Customers may review only products they have purchased.

Rules:

* Ratings are required.
* Customers may edit their own reviews.
* Reviews require administrator approval before publication.
* Administrators may reject inappropriate content.

---

# Part 3 — CMS, Notifications, Administration, Security, System Constraints & Completion

---

# 39. CMS Rules

The Content Management System controls all editable content displayed on the Customer Website.

Administrators may manage:

* Homepage Banners
* Promotional Sections
* Featured Products
* Featured Categories
* Brand Showcase
* Announcement Bar
* Homepage Collections

Rules:

* Changes take effect immediately after publishing.
* Unpublished content must never appear publicly.
* Scheduled publishing should be supported by the architecture for future enhancement.
* Every CMS modification must be recorded in the audit log.

---

# 40. Live Chat Rules

Customers may communicate with the administrator using the website's live chat.

Rules:

* Every conversation belongs to one customer or guest session.
* Conversations are timestamped.
* Messages must be stored in chronological order.
* Only administrators can close conversations.
* Closed conversations remain searchable for future reference.
* Message history must never be modified after sending.

---

# 41. Notification Rules

The system should generate notifications for significant events.

Customer notifications:

* Welcome
* Email Verification
* Password Reset
* Order Confirmation
* Payment Received
* Order Shipped
* Refund Approved

Administrator notifications:

* New Order
* Failed Payment
* Low Stock Alert
* Out of Stock Alert
* New Customer Registration
* New Review Awaiting Approval
* Inventory Adjustment
* Security Alert

Rules:

* Notifications must be generated only once per qualifying event.
* Failed notification delivery should be retried according to configurable retry policies.
* Notification failures must not interrupt the primary business transaction.

---

# 42. Reporting Rules

Reports should accurately represent historical business data.

Supported reports include:

* Sales
* Revenue
* Orders
* Inventory
* Customers
* Products
* Promotions

Rules:

* Reports are generated from authoritative database records.
* Exported reports must match on-screen values.
* Reports should support filtering by date range.

---

# 43. Analytics Rules

Dashboard analytics should calculate:

* Today's Sales
* Weekly Sales
* Monthly Sales
* Annual Sales
* Revenue
* Profit
* Conversion Rate
* Repeat Customers
* Top Categories
* Top Products
* Top Brands

Rules:

* Analytics must reflect verified data only.
* Calculations should be optimized for performance.
* Cached analytics must refresh appropriately to maintain accuracy.

---

# 44. Administrative Operation Rules

The system currently supports a single administrator role.

Administrator responsibilities include:

* Product Management
* Category Management
* Brand Management
* Inventory Management
* Order Processing
* Customer Support
* Review Moderation
* Promotion Management
* CMS Management
* Report Generation
* Store Configuration

Rules:

* Administrative actions require authentication.
* Sensitive operations require confirmation.
* Every critical action must generate an audit log entry.

The architecture should remain extensible for future RBAC expansion.

---

# 45. Security Rules

The platform must enforce:

* Role-Based Access Control architecture (future-ready)
* Two-Factor Authentication for administrators
* Session Management
* CSRF Protection
* Rate Limiting
* Device History
* Login History
* Secure HTTP Headers
* Input Validation
* Output Encoding

Rules:

* Authorization is validated on every protected request.
* Security controls must be enforced by the backend.
* Sensitive configuration values must never be exposed to clients.

---

# 46. Audit Log Rules

Audit logs must record all critical administrative events.

Examples include:

* Login
* Logout
* Product Created
* Product Updated
* Product Archived
* Inventory Adjusted
* Coupon Created
* CMS Updated
* Settings Modified
* Order Status Changed

Each record includes:

* Administrator
* Action
* Target Resource
* Timestamp
* IP Address
* Device Information (where available)

Rules:

* Audit logs are immutable.
* Audit logs are searchable.
* Audit logs cannot be edited or deleted through the application.

---

# 47. Data Retention Rules

Operational records should be preserved to maintain business continuity and auditability.

Data that should remain available includes:

* Orders
* Payments
* Inventory Movements
* Audit Logs
* Customer Accounts
* Reviews
* Support Conversations

Records should be archived rather than permanently deleted whenever practical.

---

# 48. Scheduled Job Rules

Background jobs may perform tasks such as:

* Expire Flash Sales
* Expire Coupons
* Release Reserved Inventory
* Send Scheduled Notifications
* Refresh Analytics
* Remove Expired Sessions

Scheduled tasks must be:

* Idempotent
* Logged
* Retryable
* Safe to execute multiple times

---

# 49. Global System Constraints

The platform currently operates under the following constraints:

Business:

* Single Vendor
* One Warehouse
* Nigeria-based Operations
* International Shipping Supported

Technical:

* Customer Website and Admin Dashboard are deployed independently using separate URLs.
* Both applications communicate with the same backend services and database.
* Authentication is provided by Supabase Auth.
* Payments are processed using supported payment providers.
* All pricing, inventory, and business decisions are determined by backend logic.

Future enhancements should not require redesigning the existing architecture.
